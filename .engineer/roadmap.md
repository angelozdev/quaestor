The whole database carries exactly two CHECK constraints, both on `transaction` (`ck_transaction_category_by_type`, `ck_transaction_meta_only_on_expense`), and NEITHER touches money. SCOPE: the two investment accounts and their transfers first, then a migration adding `amount > 0` across the seven columns.       Named by Angelo 2026-08-04, hours after 003 shipped, while working out how he would save for a phone. His words: "falta algo para ahorrar por algo específico... yo lo llamaría metas también". GAP: a fund cannot say what it is for. `Fund` has no name column - its identity IS its category - and `UniqueConstraint("category_id", name="uq_fund_category")` allows exactly one per category (AC-25). So saving $8.000.000 for a phone and separately for a TV, both in Tecnología, cannot be expressed. WHAT 003 TRADED: the old Goal had a name (the owner's was "Korea"). ADR-037 collapsed goals into funds to kill two real problems - the monthly contribution ritual and the coupling to a savings account (ADR-015) - and the name went out with them, unremarked. Nobody asked "what if two goals share a category" because production held exactly one goal, so no scenario forced it. Found by reasoning about use, not by any test. WHY THE OBVIOUS FIX FAILS: naming the fund and lifting the unique constraint does not work, and the reason is the model's core. A fund's balance is DERIVED from its category's posted spending; with two funds on Tecnología, a $200.000 charger has no answer to "which one did this come out of". Asking the owner per purchase reinstates exactly the ritual 003 exists to delete. That is what the one-per-category constraint is protecting, and it should stay. CONSTRAINT FROM THE OWNER: funds themselves are correct and are not to be changed - "esto de los fondos está bien, no vayas a cambiar nada". SECOND GAP, FOUND 2026-08-05 by probing the running services layer, from the other direction: a fund cannot know its purpose was FULFILLED, and cannot renew. Angelo asked whether he could save for a year toward something technological and have it restart when the year ends - possibly asking him whether to raise the amount or move the date. SCOPE: `amount_min`/`amount_max` + currency and `group_id` through the four doors (service, REST, MCP, UI), the row action on the three screens, and a filter bar that survives having nine controls instead of seven - which is where the 'user friendly' in the request actually lands. Only the group filter needs the drill-down to exist first; the amount filter is independent and could ship alone.
