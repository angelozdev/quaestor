- [ ] **Name what each fund rule DOES, so the owner can find the tool he is asking for** `id:fund-vs-budget-vocabulary` priority:1 status:planned area:budget → feature:—
      Surfaced 2026-08-05 during the named-goals discuss, from the strongest possible evidence: the OWNER of the app asked for a feature that is already built and shipped, and did not know it existed. His words: 'es que estos son presupuestos, me parece. O sea, esos no son fondos sino presupuestos' - describing a monthly ceiling on Restaurantes that does not roll over. That is exactly a fund with `accumulates=false`, which the API, the domain fold (`fund_next_opening_calc`) and the create form all support today. THE GAP IS NAMING, NOT BEHAVIOUR. The only surface the concept has is one unlabelled consequence - a checkbox reading 'Acumula lo que sobra cada mes' (frontend/app/(app)/funds/page.tsx:293) - with no explanation of what ticking it changes, no name for either resulting shape, and the word 'presupuesto' appearing NOWHERE in the product. So the app ships two genuinely different money tools under one noun and lets the owner discover the second one by accident, or not at all. WHAT THE TWO SHAPES ARE, in his own numbers: unticked = Tecnologia 100.000/mes as a CEILING, spend 60.000 and the 40.000 is gone, September restarts at 100.000. Ticked = Tecnologia 100.000/mes as an ENVELOPE, spend 60.000 and September opens at 140.000, which is what irregular costs (the annual insurance, the repair) need. SCOPE: this is a product-vocabulary and UI decision, not a model change - nothing in the fold moves. Candidate shape: the create form asks which of the two you want in plain Spanish BEFORE asking for a number, each with a one-line consequence, and the funds table says which one each fund is. Whether the two get separate names in the product (presupuesto / fondo) or stay one noun with a stated mode is the decision to take, and it needs a product ADR because ADR-037 deliberately collapsed nouns and this would partly re-expand them. RELATED: 003's own spec never states the difference in owner-facing words either, so whatever is decided likely earns an AC. Does NOT block named-goals, but the metas screen will have to name this difference anyway to explain why a meta is not a fund - so doing it first is cheaper than doing it twice. THIRD PIECE OF EVIDENCE, same conversation, minutes later: the owner asked whether he could save month by month to pay an ANNUAL SUBSCRIPTION, and whether that was even possible. It is the `from-recurring` rule, shipped, tested and in production, labelled 'Lo que piden sus obligaciones' in the create form (page.tsx:28) - a label that names a mechanism and not a use, so nothing about it says 'this is how you pay for Netflix once a year'. With a 600.000 yearly charge due in March, from August the fund asks 85.715 a month, and `_charge_month_for` rolls it straight on to the NEXT cycle the month the charge is settled (AC-11), so it renews itself forever with no owner action - strictly better than a meta for anything recurring. THAT MAKES THREE IN ONE SESSION: the ceiling (accumulates=false), the envelope (accumulates=true) and the subscription filler (from-recurring), all built, all invisible. The pattern is not one bad checkbox - it is that all four rules are named after the ARITHMETIC they run ('Monto fijo', 'Promedio de los ultimos meses', 'Lo que piden sus obligaciones', 'Meta con fecha') and never after the JOB the owner came to do. The owner is the person who commissioned every one of them and could not find three. RAISED TO PRIORITY 1: this outranks building anything new, because new features inherit the same naming and become invisible too. SCOPE GROWS ACCORDINGLY: not just the accumulate checkbox but the whole rule picker - each option stating, in the owner's words and with a number, what it is for. No behaviour moves; the fold is untouched.

- [ ] **Backend errors become codes the frontend maps to Spanish copy** `id:error-contract` priority:2 status:planned area:core → feature:—
      Requested by Angelo 2026-08-04, right after browser QA on the dev sandbox surfaced it. TRIGGER: creating a category that already exists shows the owner a red toast reading "an expense category named 'Transporte' already exists". The app is Spanish throughout; the backend is not. This is not one string - every server refusal reaches the UI in English, and the same defect put a raw cents figure on screen (a fund warning printed 100000000 next to a form where the owner had typed 1000000, a 100x difference at the exact moment the app was asking him to reconsider). THE GAP IS A DECISION NOBODY MADE: ADR-0001 fixed English for all code and explicitly put user-facing copy OUT of scope - "those are a localization concern and may be authored in any language or routed through i18n" - and no ADR ever picked up what it put down. DECISION BY THE OWNER, taken over the alternative recommended to him (server writes Spanish directly): the backend emits stable error CODES plus the data they need, and the frontend maps code to copy. His reason: the frontend should be able to map them correctly. THE SEAM ALREADY EXISTS AND IS ONE LINE - `api/errors.py:59` returns `str(exc)` straight to the wire, and MCP does NOT pass through it (it has its own `mcp/format.py:domain_error_text` for agent text), so the two audiences are already separated and the agent surface must not be touched. SIZE, measured not guessed: 102 raise sites across services/ and domain/, 69 distinct messages, concentrated in categories (21), recurring (17), transactions (14), planned (13), funds (11). SCOPE: an ADR first (API/transport design, CLAUDE.md requires one); what a code carries besides its name - the interpolated values are the point, since "category 'X' already exists" and "it would ask 1000000" only work if the name and the amount travel as data for the client to format with `formatCents`; whether the code registry lives in one module or beside each raise; the frontend catalog and its fallback for an unknown code; `ApiError.fields` already exists and already drives inline form errors, so field-level mapping is half-built. TWO THINGS THE CODES DO NOT COVER, both needing an answer in the same ADR: Pydantic's own validator messages ("field required") reach the user through `_format_validation` and come from the library, not from a raise; and the fund's implausible-target warning is NOT an exception at all - it is a `FundPreview.warning` string in a normal 200 response whose number already travels beside it as `would_ask`, so it is the cheapest possible pilot for the whole pattern and should be done first as a proof. FILED AS C21 in consolidation.
- [ ] **A fund can be told what it already holds, from the app, for a stated month** `id:fund-opening-balance` priority:2 status:planned area:budget → feature:—
      Decided by Angelo 2026-08-05 as product ADR-041, closing ADR-0043's anchor amendment; filed as consolidation C23. TWO GAPS, ONE FIX. (1) The fund form has no opening-balance field and the table has no edit action, so a stated balance is reachable ONLY through the assistant - the API carries it on both create and update, the screen carries it on neither. (2) What the assistant writes through `set_fund(balance=...)` carries NO month, and the fold then reads it as "stated for whichever month is being looked at", so the figure follows the owner across months: told in January that a fund holds $500.000 while it asks $200.000 a month, February should open at $700.000 and December at $0, and the app answers $500.000 to all three - inventing money in the past. THE RULE: a stated balance always carries a month and never a freely chosen one. Said while creating, it carries the fund's start month, which the owner just picked on the same screen. Said afterwards through the assistant, it carries the current month. Never backdated. VERIFIED AGAINST PRACTICE before deciding: neither YNAB nor Actual Budget has an undated category balance - the budget IS month-by-month, money is assigned into a month's column and the balance rolls from there, so there is nothing to date because nothing floats; YNAB refuses retroactive resets outright, a Fresh Start begins today and history is reflected by entering movements. SCOPE: the form field dated at start month, the current-month stamp on the update path, and scenarios for the anchor branch - mutation left three survivors there (`stated_for > year_month` inverted plus both of its return values) precisely because no scenario dates an anchor at all, and they stop being a hole once there is a decided behaviour to pin. `create_fund(opening_balance=...)` already dates correctly and needs nothing.
- [ ] **Metas: several named funds per category, each saving for a specific thing** `id:named-goals` priority:2 status:in-progress area:budget → feature:named-goals
      Named by Angelo 2026-08-04, hours after 003 shipped, while working out how he would save for a phone. His words: "falta algo para ahorrar por algo específico... yo lo llamaría metas también". GAP: a fund cannot say what it is for. `Fund` has no name column - its identity IS its category - and `UniqueConstraint("category_id", name="uq_fund_category")` allows exactly one per category (AC-25). So saving $8.000.000 for a phone and separately for a TV, both in Tecnología, cannot be expressed. WHAT 003 TRADED: the old Goal had a name (the owner's was "Korea"). ADR-037 collapsed goals into funds to kill two real problems - the monthly contribution ritual and the coupling to a savings account (ADR-015) - and the name went out with them, unremarked. Nobody asked "what if two goals share a category" because production held exactly one goal, so no scenario forced it. Found by reasoning about use, not by any test. WHY THE OBVIOUS FIX FAILS: naming the fund and lifting the unique constraint does not work, and the reason is the model's core. A fund's balance is DERIVED from its category's posted spending; with two funds on Tecnología, a $200.000 charger has no answer to "which one did this come out of". Asking the owner per purchase reinstates exactly the ritual 003 exists to delete. That is what the one-per-category constraint is protecting, and it should stay. LIKELY SHAPE: a second noun beside the fund, not a change to it. A fund is automatic - category spending drives it, no input required. A meta would be deliberate - named, and the owner says "I put $300.000 toward the TV" - so it needs its own contribution surface and does not read category spending at all. That distinction is the thing to design; it is close to what the old Goal was, minus the account coupling and minus the month-close hook. WORKAROUND UNTIL THEN: one category per savings target ("Celular nuevo"), which works today at zero cost in code but mixes "how I classify spending" with "what I am buying" and leaves dead categories behind once the thing is bought. CONSTRAINT FROM THE OWNER: funds themselves are correct and are not to be changed - "esto de los fondos está bien, no vayas a cambiar nada". SECOND GAP, FOUND 2026-08-05 by probing the running services layer, from the other direction: a fund cannot know its purpose was FULFILLED, and cannot renew. Angelo asked whether he could save for a year toward something technological and have it restart when the year ends - possibly asking him whether to raise the amount or move the date. The probe: a fund targeting 8.000.000 COP by 2027-05 asks 727.273 a month, reaches the target, then asks 0 every month FOREVER while still occupying its category (the one-per-category constraint means no other fund can use Tecnologia meanwhile), and the month the money is actually spent it asks the FULL 8.000.000 again - because the fold reads the spending as a setback, not as the purchase the fund existed for. So the same missing noun shows up twice from two unrelated directions: a fund cannot say WHAT it is for (naming), and cannot say it is DONE (lifecycle). A meta needs a completion state and an answer to 'what now' - close it, renew it with a new date, or renew it with a new amount.

- [ ] **Daily job that fetches the USD→COP rate so it is never missing** `id:daily-trm-fetch` priority:3 status:planned area:core → feature:—
      Named by Angelo 2026-08-04 as the expiry of product ADR-038: "la tasa se aplica al entrar en la app, siempre debe estar (mientras creamos un feature que obtenga la TRM por debajo día a día)". ADR-038 makes the rate a hard prerequisite of every read path - the money available, the fund status, the rates, the monthly report and the outstanding queue all refuse to render without one, even for a month recorded entirely in pesos. Until it ships, setting the rate is a manual one-time act on a fresh install.
