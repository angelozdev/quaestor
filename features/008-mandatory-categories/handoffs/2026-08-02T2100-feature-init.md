# feature-init — handoff summary

## What I did

Created `features/008-mandatory-categories/` at `status: ready`,
`autonomy_level: medium`, from the `feature_intake` produced by the 2026-08-02
`discuss` session on `003-sinking-funds`. Branch `mandatory-categories` created
off `main`.

## Intake

- **Title:** Category becomes mandatory on every expense and income
- **Outcome:** every expense and income carries a category, enforced at the
  write path and pinned by a migration; transfers carry none by rule; no
  historical row left uncategorised
- **Source:** split out of the sinking-funds discuss, where `from-recurring`
  funding rules could only derive 3 funds from recurring items instead of 8
- **Autonomy:** `medium` — within `manifest.autonomy.allowed_levels`
  (`[low, medium]`). Drops to `low` automatically on
  `backend/src/quaestor/migrations/**` per the manifest path override.

## Decomposition check

Single competency (domain rule + backfill + migration), one PR's worth. Kept as
one feature. It was itself the decomposition — carved out of 003 precisely so
that feature does not absorb it.

## Why it is separate, and first

- **Independent value.** It pays off with or without sinking funds: today
  $2.072.854 COP + US$7.486,68 of spending and $7.003.101 COP + US$10.495,55 of
  income sit outside every per-category report, average and budget.
- **Small.** One rule, one backfill, one migration.
- **It improves 003's best rule.** With categories set, `from-recurring`
  derives 8 funds instead of 3, and Internet resolves to its exact $85.000
  recurring amount rather than a $149.585 three-month average.

## The one nuance carried into the feature

The rule is *not* blanket NOT NULL. Transfers must stay uncategorised — a
transfer between the owner's own accounts is not spending, and categorising one
would count the same money twice (once into Emergency Fund, again when spent out
of it). All 39 existing transfers are correctly null. The constraint has to
discriminate by transaction type.
