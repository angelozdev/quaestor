## The four that mattered

- **D1 — a door said "created" for something the rule forbids.** `TransferIn`
  and MCP's `TransferInput` carried no category field, so Pydantic's
  `extra="ignore"` dropped it and a transfer requesting a category returned 201.
  The stored row was still correct, so nothing was corrupted; what was wrong is
  that the caller was told yes. AC-3's scenario passed the whole time because
  its handler calls `transactions.transfer()` directly — a place no client can
  reach. That is the kind of green that proves nothing.

- **D2 — a category's direction could be flipped under the movements filed
  under it.** No guard on `update_category(is_income=...)`, reachable from
  REST, MCP and the "Es ingreso" checkbox. File twenty expenses under Comida,
  tick the box, and all twenty carry an income category — the exact
  contradiction ADR-0042 exists to remove, and feature 003 is sequenced behind
  reading those averages.

- **N1/N2 — two correct rules composing into a dead end.** With uniqueness
  global, "Intereses" as an expense meant the app would neither offer it when
  recording bank interest (the list is direction-filtered) nor let you create
  it (the name exists). It declined to offer and refused to create, in the same
  breath — while AC-15's own refusal advised filing under the other direction.
  The owner ruled the name space is per direction.

- **N7 — the feature's advice produced the duplicate the feature exists to
  eliminate.** Detailed in the verify handoff; it lands on the exact production
  pair (`🛡️ Auto Insurance`) that acs.md and ADR-0042 both cite as motivation.
