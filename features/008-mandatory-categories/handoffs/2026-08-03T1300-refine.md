new guard entirely, and returned a success card for a restore that never
happened. Commit 8cd0662 states the guard is reachable from both doors. It was
reachable from one.

interpolated the row being restored instead of the row blocking it, and
matching folds case.

Both are detailed with repro paths in the verify handoff.
