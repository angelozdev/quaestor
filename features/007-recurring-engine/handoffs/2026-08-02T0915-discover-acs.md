# discover-acs — handoff summary

## What I did

Ran Checkpoint 2 in the clean-room mode the user fixed at intake: drafted the
criteria from product behaviour first, took the decisions the product left open,
and only then reopened `services/recurring.py`, `domain/rules.due_dates`, the
REST router and the movements page to diff shipped behaviour against them.

Eight product decisions were taken with Angelo. One of them — whether backfilled
charges may land inside an already-closed month — was answered by checking
rather than deciding: `services/rollover.close_month` freezes nothing, so no
closed-month restriction entered the criteria. Decision 1 (per-date acceptance
of past due dates) was taken against industry precedent: Firefly III, Actual
Budget and YNAB all refuse backfill outright; GnuCash backfills through its
*Since Last Run* review list, which is the pattern AC-12 adopts.

## Findings

- **Nine divergences, all resolved as fix.** Seven decided by the user (AC-6,
  12, 13, 17, 20, 24, 25); two proposed by the diff and still unratified
  (AC-21 skipping a date the item never falls due on, AC-22 an account archived
  after declaration that keeps being charged).
- **The four intake candidates all landed as fixes**, and the diff found five
  more the intake had not seen — the whole-batch rollback (AC-24), the
  pause-that-only-defers (AC-17), incomes with no resolvable surface (AC-6), and
  the two above.
- **Highest-cost defect: AC-24.** `materialize_due` rolls the entire batch back
  on any failure, so one unchargeable obligation costs every other obligation
  its day, silently, every day, until someone notices. Nothing in the product
  reports it.
- **Second: AC-12 + AC-25 together.** The engine backfills every passed due date
  on the first daily run after declaration, in automatic mode moving the balance
  for all of them at once, and the resulting movements are stored as
  hand-entered — so there is no screen on which the user can see what happened
  or why the balance moved.
- **AC-20 is a live inconsistency**, not a hypothetical: skipping a date already
  charged marks the occurrence skipped and leaves the money out of the account.
- **Seam found with feature 006:** a manual recurring income materializes a
  planned income that 006's AC-15 keeps out of the to-pay queue, so it can never
  be confirmed. Closed here by forcing incomes to automatic (AC-6); the `Por
  cobrar` view the user also wants is parked for its own `discuss`.
- **`Source.manual` is a data lie** for engine-created movements — the enum has
  `manual`, `agent`, `import` and no value for the engine. Relevant to AC-25 at
  plan time; likely an ADR.
- **Method note for the record:** the session had already read the service at
  Checkpoint 1.5, so the clean room was procedural, not memory-clean. Stated in
  `acs.md` rather than hidden. A future clean-room checkpoint should start in a
  fresh session.
