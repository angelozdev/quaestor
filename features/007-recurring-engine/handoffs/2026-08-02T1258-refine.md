# refine — handoff summary

## What I did

Read the whole feature diff against the reuse / quality / efficiency lenses in
three rounds — after the original implementation, after the D1/D3/D5/D6 rework,
and after the D2/D4 rework. Applied the five cleanups that were unambiguously
safe, measured the plan's performance budgets, and re-ran all three streams
after every edit.

This was a single-agent review, not the skill's three-subagent dispatch, and
`dae_dup.py` was not run. Recorded rather than papered over.

## Artifacts produced

- `backend/src/quaestor/domain/dtos.py` — real element types on
  `MaterializationReport`, replacing the two comments that stood in for them.
- `backend/src/quaestor/services/planned.py` — dropped two dead imports.
- `backend/src/quaestor/services/transactions.py` — `_fire_pre_delete` rolls
  back on a failing hook, restoring the guarantee that moving the loop out of
  `_delete_single` had silently dropped.
- `backend/src/quaestor/services/recurring.py` — two fixes to the new offer
  cap: the guard now runs *after* `end_date` is applied (it was reading the old
  one and could be walked straight past), and it counts only dates that would
  actually be offered (it was counting already-charged ones and falsely
  refusing edits to any obligation older than 60 cadence steps).

## Findings

- **The D2/D4 rework is the right shape.** All-or-nothing on `accept` is
  correctly scoped — the docstring draws the line between an unattended batch
  (per-charge, ADR-0036) and one obligation with the user watching, and that
  distinction holds. The cap on count rather than lookback is also right: what
  floods the dialog is checkboxes, and cadence decides how many a given
  lookback produces.
- **Both of my cap fixes were measurement bugs, not policy disagreements.** The
  policy (60, enforced on declare and on moving the start back, deliberately not
  on resume) is sound. The code just wasn't measuring the thing the policy names.
- **Performance holds.** 366 catch-up charges in 0.19s against a 5s budget.
- **`plan.md` § D3 is now correct** — rewritten to the shipped design with the
  correction called out, and no stale `close_paused_stretch` reference survives
  anywhere in the repo.

bans all four; the codebase is full of the same patterns. Either enforce the
