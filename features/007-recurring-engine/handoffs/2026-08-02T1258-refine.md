## Findings

- **The D2/D4 rework is the right shape.** All-or-nothing on `accept` is
  correctly scoped — the docstring draws the line between an unattended batch
  (per-charge, ADR-0036) and one obligation with the user watching, and that
  distinction holds. The cap on count rather than lookback is also right: what
  floods the dialog is checkboxes, and cadence decides how many a given
  lookback produces.
- **Both of my cap fixes were measurement bugs, not policy disagreements.** The
  policy (60, enforced on declare and on moving the start back, deliberately not
  on resume) is sound. The code just wasn't measuring the thing the policy names.
- **Performance holds.** 366 catch-up charges in 0.19s against a 5s budget.
- **`plan.md` § D3 is now correct** — rewritten to the shipped design with the
  correction called out, and no stale `close_paused_stretch` reference survives
  anywhere in the repo.
