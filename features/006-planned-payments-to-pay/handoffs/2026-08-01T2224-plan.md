## Findings

**No schema change.** All three behaviours are service-layer: a new
`skipped -> planned` transition, a type filter on the queue, and a closing
total line in the conversational answer. `domain/planned.py` is untouched —
what enters the two buckets is policy at the construction site, which is where
ADR-0023 already put the buckets' mutual exclusion.

**Safe-to-spend is outside the blast radius.** Traced before proposing:
`services/budgets.py` computes `committed` from `agg.month_planned_expense`, a
different path that was already expense-only. The queue filter therefore
touches three consumers — the screen, the chat, and the monthly report's
pending line — and the report's shift is the coherent one, since "pending"
means what is owed.

**ADR-0034 exists because the P3 design called skipping a cancellation.**
Restoring a payment also has to restore its recurring occurrence: that
occurrence is the marker stopping `materialize_due` from recreating the due
date, so leaving the two out of step would produce a restored payment the
recurring machinery does not recognise. The ADR also records why "plan it
again from the skipped row" was rejected — a new row is a new identity, and
the orphaned occurrence would still block the date.

**Restore lives on the transaction list, not on "Por pagar".** Angelo's call.
Skipped rows are already visible and status-filterable there, so it is the
cheapest surface; and a skipped payment is no longer debt, so keeping it out
of the outstanding screen protects the headline total.

**Phases F1–F3 are independent** — different functions of the same module,
no red scenario depends on another. F4 (frontend) depends on F1.
