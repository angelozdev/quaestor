# discover-acs — handoff summary

## What I did

Reverse-engineered 24 acceptance criteria for the planned-payments queue from
the shipped surface and 30 existing tests (service, wire, value-object and
component layers), the P3 temporal-engine design, ADR-0023/0031/0032 and the
migration that corrected planned-confirm transfer directions. Then interviewed
Angelo on the five questions the material left genuinely undecided.

## Artifacts produced

- `features/006-planned-payments-to-pay/acs.md` (24 ACs, 14 high priority)
- `features/006-planned-payments-to-pay/feature.md` (Scope + Notes updated for
  the two behaviours that broadened scope)

## Findings

**Defect found during discovery — planned incomes inflate what is owed.**
`services.planned.to_pay` filters on status alone, and the queue total sums
amounts without regard to type. A manually-recurring income materializes a
planned income, so a 5.000.000 salary next to an 85.000 phone bill renders
"Por pagar 5.085.000". Angelo chose to fix it inside this feature: AC-15 says
the queue carries only obligations. The retrospective read used by the monthly
report shares the same path — check that seam at plan time.

**New behaviour requested — undo a skip.** No `skipped -> planned` transition
exists today; a mis-skip is unrecoverable except by re-planning by hand. AC-8
adds it. Needs an ADR at Checkpoint 4 (it changes the state contract that P3
described as terminal).

**Feature 005's fail-loud rate contract upheld.** Angelo initially asked for a
default exchange rate so the queue would never fail. Surfacing the conflict
with feature 005's AC-9 — and the concrete consequence, a USD 1.000 obligation
rendering as COP 100.000 under an arbitrary rate of 100 — reversed the call.
AC-20 documents the current behaviour unchanged; no ADR supersede needed.

**Two questions resolved as no-change.** Resolving something twice stays a
refusal rather than becoming idempotent (AC-18), and planning a one-off income
stays out of scope — recurring incomes already cover the repeating case, and
forecasting inflows is a separate conversation.

**Reconciliation debt (reverse-engineer mode).** `acs.md` now states two
behaviours the shipped code does not have (AC-8, AC-15) and the pre-DAE P3
design still describes `to_pay` returning a flat `{items, total_base}` shape
that ADR-0023 replaced. Both are `consistency-check` targets.
