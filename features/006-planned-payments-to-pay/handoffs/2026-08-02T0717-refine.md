## What I applied

**Backend source — `services/planned.py`**

1. **`OBLIGATION_TYPES` -> `_OBLIGATION_TYPES`.** It reads as a public
   constant but has exactly two references, both two lines apart in the
   same module (grep-verified across `backend/` and `acceptance/`).
2. **`_move_status` parameters renamed** `expected`/`to`/`occurrence_to` ->
   `from_status`/`to_status`/`occurrence_status`. `expected` named a
   precondition while `to` named a target — a mismatched pair, and bare
   `to=` was too terse for one of three keyword-only args.
3. **`_move_status` docstring corrected.** It claimed "Move a tx between two
   non-posting states" — an invariant the function does not enforce
   (`to_status=TxStatus.posted` would compile and silently post without
   balance movement or hooks). It now says what it does and why
   `confirm_payment` stays out: "Moves no balance and fires no hooks —
   `confirm_payment` owns the only path to `posted`."
4. **`to_pay` bucket naming.** The implementer renamed `upcoming_items` ->
   `upcoming_rows` purely to free the name, leaving `overdue_items` (also
   pre-filter) beside it, with `_obligations` deferred to the `return`.
   Both locals now mean "final bucket contents" — `_obligations` applies at
   each branch and the return is a plain `from_lists(overdue_items,
   upcoming_items)`. This reverts the rename churn rather than adding to it.
5. **Stale docstring pointers fixed.** `to_pay`'s docstring cited
   `services/goals.py:216` and `services/reports.py:215`. Verified against
   the current tree: the `today` kwarg is at `goals.py:215` and
   `reports.py:187` — the second pointer lands 28 lines away, mid-constructor.
   Now symbol names: "the same pattern as `goals.goals_progress` and
   `reports.monthly_report`", which cannot go stale on an unrelated edit.

## What I rejected, and on which rule

**Rejected — `test_builder.py` / `test_tool_tiers.py` inventory triplication
(the implementer's flagged candidate 3). Escalated to a human ADR decision.**

The reuse lens made a strong, mechanically-sound case: add
`ALL_TOOL_NAMES` to `registry.py` as the union of the 13 group tuples and
point both hand-maintained lists at it (-110/+18). I verified the mechanism
is viable — all 13 constants really are 1-tuples, not bare strings (checked
by AST, and `assert len(REPORTS_TOOL_NAMES) == 1` passes) — and that
`test_registry.py` already derives from those tuples in 12 places, so the
precedent exists in the same directory. The `test_tool_tiers.py` half would
even be a *strengthening*: today you can add a tool to `TEMPORAL_TOOL_NAMES`
and forget `_KNOWN_TOOL_NAMES`, and the tier test silently stops covering it.

I still did not apply it. **`CLAUDE.md`'s technical-decisions rule names
"testing strategy" explicitly as ADR-requiring**, and this changes what the
test suite *guarantees* about the LLM-reachable tool surface — the ADR-0020
tier boundary that keeps `restore_payment` out of `LLM_ALLOWED_TOOLS`. Going
from three independent hand-maintained restatements to one derived source of
truth is precisely a testing-strategy decision on a security-adjacent guard,
and on this project ADRs are human-gated (ADR-0034 is sitting at `proposed`
for the same reason). A refactor that could silently weaken that guard is not
something to take autonomously at medium autonomy. **This is a real
improvement worth having — it wants an ADR and a human, not a refiner.**

**Rejected — move `OBLIGATION_TYPES` into `domain/planned.py`** (reuse lens).
Charter §1: ADRs and the approved plan are respected, never silently
contradicted. `plan.md` states `domain/planned.py` stays **intacto** and that
"qué entra en esas listas es política del sitio de construcción" per
ADR-0023. Contradicting an approved plan at CP6 is out of bounds. Took only
the private rename.

**Rejected — delete "the same figure the app's headline shows" from
`to_pay_table`'s docstring** (quality lens argued it is an unverifiable
cross-layer claim). I checked and it is true *by construction*, not by
coincidence: `api/routers/planned.py:23` builds `total_base` from
`queue.total_cop_cents(trm)` and `format.py` calls the same method. That
shared method is exactly what makes ADR-0006/0009 parity hold, and the clause
documents the requirement AC-24 encodes. With comments banned, the docstring
is the only place that *why* can live.

**Rejected — split `to_pay` into `_overdue_bucket` / `_upcoming_bucket`**
(the implementer's flagged candidate 1). Both the quality and efficiency
lenses independently said leave it. The body is 30 lines; the two queries are
*defined by each other*, and `domain/planned.py`'s module docstring states
the mutual-exclusion invariant "is preserved at the call site, not enforced
at the VO". Splitting the call site into two independent functions is
precisely what would let that disjointness drift. Length is not the defect.
Nor did I trim the docstring: comments are banned project-wide, so it is the
only place the `until`-constrains-overdue rule, the obligations-only rule and
the `today`-for-testability rationale can live. Only the stale line numbers
were wrong, and those are fixed.

**Rejected — `_move_status` is a "parameterised twin"** (the implementer's
flagged candidate 1, second half). It de-duplicates the *entire* body of both
operations — twelve lines, not a fragment — and ADR-0034 states restore is
the exact inverse of skip, so a shared helper makes that inverse-ness
structural rather than aspirational. That `confirm_payment` deliberately does
*not* use it is evidence the boundary is drawn correctly. Kept; only the
names and docstring needed work.

**Rejected — rename `show?` or hoist the `actions.filter()` out of the JSX**
(the implementer's flagged candidate 4). The seam is right. `DataTable` has
exactly one consumer today (`transactions/page.tsx`), and the two pages that
hand-roll conditional actions (`accounts`, `recurring`) both do
mutually-exclusive pairs, which `show` expresses as two inverse predicates —
strictly the more general seam. `DropdownMenuContent` renders inside a
Portal, so the filter only evaluates while a menu is open, over an array of
three: no performance case, and `useMemo` here would be premature. On naming,
grep found no predicate-prop convention anywhere in `frontend/components/` or
`frontend/ui/` to conform to; `hidden` would invert the absent-means-visible
default into a double negative at the call site.

**Rejected on scope — merging `_planned_income` into `_planned_transfer`,
and replacing the two inline occurrence-query blocks at `test_planned.py`
262-267 / 444-448.** All pre-existing code outside this feature's diff.
Touching it would grow the review surface for cosmetic gain.

**Rejected on scope — the dead `from decimal import Decimal` inside
`_planned_transfer`.** Same dead-import defect as the one I removed, but
pre-existing. Deleted only the one this feature introduced.

## Findings for the team lead — reported, not changed

**1. `plan.md`'s performance budget contains a claim the code does not
honour.** `plan.md` lines 150-151 state the combined total "se calcula sobre
las filas ya convertidas, **sin segunda pasada de conversión**". It is not
true: `total_cop_cents(trm)` re-walks and re-converts every row that
`_to_pay_rows` already converted per section. The *cost* is noise (tens of
microseconds at the plan's stated tens-of-rows, and only USD rows do Decimal
work — `to_cop_cents` short-circuits COP to a plain int), and the API path at
`api/routers/planned.py:21-23` already does the identical double pass, so
optimising only the MCP side would make the two surfaces inconsistent for
zero gain. I did not edit `plan.md`: it is an approved CP4 artifact and
amending it is the architect's call, not the refiner's. **Decision needed:
amend the budget line to say the total re-uses `total_cop_cents`, or leave
it.**

**2. Latent gap in `data-table.tsx`, flagged by two lenses independently.**
Lines 96 and 114 gate the actions column on `actions.length > 0`, not on the
per-row *visible* count. A future row where every action is `show`-gated off
would still render a `MoreHorizontal` trigger opening an empty dropdown. Not
reachable today (`Editar` and `Eliminar` have no `show`). Fixing it changes
behaviour, so it is out of bounds for refine — recording it so the next
person to add a `show` does not rediscover it.

**3. "Planned money out" now means two different things.** The queue counts
expense + transfer (`_obligations`), while `budgets._committed` and
`month_aggregate.month_planned_expense` count expense only. Both are
intentional per their docstrings and unifying them would change behaviour —
but the divergence is worth a sentence in ADR-0034 rather than a refactor.
Worth a CP7 look.
