## 1 — The step that says «the user views the funds» reads the month once per fund

**Rank:** 1 · **Lens:** efficiency, then quality · **Churn:** medium · **Verified**

**What.** `sinking_funds.when_view_funds` was rewritten this feature to call
`_read_the_funds_screen`, which lists the funds and then calls `fund_status`
once for each — and every `fund_status` calls `load_month` again. Measured with
five funded categories:

| | five funds | one fund |
|---|---|---|
| `available()` — what `GET /funds/available` calls, and what the screen loads | 14 statements | 14 |
| the step `the user views the funds` | **77 statements** | 17 |

So AC-17's scenario reads:

```
When the user views the funds                         → 77 statements, O(funds)
Then the month was read once, not once for each fund  → measures available(), 14 = 14
```

The `Then` discards what the `When` did and measures a different call. The
criterion is genuinely met by the app — `available` is flat, because
`obligations_in` is an in-memory filter over `active_recurring` and nothing in
`fold` touches the session — but the step that claims to model the screen is
modelling something the screen does not do, and it is the very shape AC-17
forbids.

**Why.** The screen calls `moneyAvailable(month)` (`page.tsx:315-318`), not
`fund_status` per row. Reading `available` in that step would model the screen,
cost 14 statements instead of 77, and let AC-17's `Then` measure the path its
own `When` took instead of substituting one.

**Blast radius.** One function in `acceptance/handlers/sinking_funds.py`. The
step appears in 19 scenarios — 3 in `features/003-sinking-funds/spec.md`, 16 in
this feature's. The per-fund assertions go through `sinking_funds._status`,
which calls `fund_status` on its own, so they are unaffected by what the `When`
loaded.

**Note.** The `Then` step's habit of deleting funds inside an assertion — the
thing I was asked to look hard at — is *not* the problem here. The database is
per-scenario, it is the last step, and the deletion is the measurement. Its real
defect is that it measures a path the `When` never took.

## 2 — `preview_fund` walks the category's obligations a third time for an answer it already holds

**Rank:** 2 · **Lens:** reuse, efficiency · **Churn:** small · **Verified**

**What.** Instrumented, one `preview_fund` on the obligations rule calls
`_obligations` **3 times** and `next_due_on_or_after` **9 times**. Two passes are
`_walk`'s (the month's ask, and `ask_before_spending`). The third is this line:

```python
has_something_to_spread=any(o.can_be_spread for o in _obligations(agg, category_id, start))
```

For the obligations rule that third pass is provably the same list the charges
were built from: `_ask_from_obligations` builds `charges` by zipping
`_obligations(agg, fund.category_id, year_month)` with `claim_holdings` under
`strict=True`, so the two are one-to-one and in order, and
`any(c.can_be_spread for c in walked.ask.charges)` is the identical answer at
zero cost.

**The catch, stated honestly.** For a `fixed` or `average` preview `charges` is
empty, so deriving it that way would report `has_something_to_spread=False`
where today it reports the truth — measured `True` on a `fixed` preview of a
category holding a yearly charge. Nothing reads it there: `offerTheRule`
short-circuits on `rule !== "from-recurring"`, the `fixed` rule never previews
at all (`previewBody` returns `null` for it), and `mcp/format.py:369`
`fund_preview_card` prints only `would_ask` and `warning` — grepped, the MCP
surface never names either new field. So this narrows a wire field to "answered
for the rule it is about", which is a contract narrowing worth writing into the
`FundPreview` docstring, not a UI change.

**Blast radius.** `preview_fund` and one docstring sentence in `dtos.py`.

## 3 — A monthly charge in its final month still fires the warning

**Rank:** 3 · **Lens:** quality (a defect, reported not fixed) · **Churn:** small code, but it needs a scenario · **Verified** · **Owner's call — this is a behaviour change and refine must not make it**

**What.** `_can_be_spread` answers yes when no turn follows the charge:

```python
following = _turn_after(item, charge_month)
return following is None or months_to_fund(charge_month, following) > 1
```

The docstring says that branch is for a one-off — "a charge with no turn after
it is a one-off, and nothing forces it to be asked in a single month". But a
*monthly* charge whose `end_date` sits on its last turn also has no turn
following it, so it answers yes too, becomes `crowded`, and warns. Driven
against a real session:

| the charge | `has_something_to_spread` | warning |
|---|---|---|
| EPM 250.000 monthly, no end date | False | none — correct, AC-13 |
| EPM 250.000 monthly, `end_date` 2026-08-05 (its last turn) | True | **`EPM charges in 2026-08, which leaves no month to save in: the whole 250000.00 COP falls on 2026-08`** |
| EPM 250.000 monthly, `end_date` 2026-09-30 | False | none — correct |
| Lavado 50.000 weekly | False | none — correct |

AC-13 is categorical: *«Un cobro mensual nunca dispara el aviso»*. `end_date` is
settable from the recurring screen (`frontend/app/(app)/recurring/page.tsx:289`
and `:536`), so this is reachable, not theoretical. It is the exact defect
ADR-0054 was written to kill, surviving in a narrower shape.

**Why it matters more than its rarity suggests.** ADR-0054's whole claim is that
AC-13 stopped being a condition someone must remember to check and became a
consequence of the definition. This is the definition leaking: "no turn after
it" is standing in for "it is a one-off", and the two are not the same
predicate.

**Blast radius.** `_can_be_spread`, plus one scenario under AC-13 in `spec.md`
— which needs the owner's permission before anyone touches it.

## 4 — The render-phase form write converges, but React warns, and no test goes near it

**Rank:** 4 · **Lens:** quality · **Churn:** small · **Verified**

**What.** `create-form.tsx:152-154` writes to the form store while
`CreateFundForm` is rendering:

```tsx
if (offeredRules.length > 0 && !offeredRules.includes(values.rule)) {
  form.setFieldValue("rule", offeredRules[0])
}
```

**It cannot loop.** `offerTheRule` returns `true` unconditionally for any rule
that is not `from-recurring`, and `rulesFor("fondo")` always contains `fixed`,
so `offeredRules[0]` is always an offered rule and the condition is false on the
next render. That answers the convergence question.

**It does warn.** I drove the only path that reaches it — choose a category with
something spreadable, select the subscription rule, then switch to a category
with only monthly charges — and React logged:

```
Cannot update a component (`Field`) while rendering a different component
(`CreateFundForm`). To locate the bad setState() call inside `CreateFundForm`,
follow the stack trace as described in https://react.dev/link/setstate-in-render
```

The rule *does* switch to `fixed` and the form stays usable. But the store write
notifies the already-mounted `form.Field` subscribers synchronously, which is
the render-phase update React is objecting to.

**Nothing in CI sees it.** Both AC-14 tests (`page.test.tsx`, "014 AC-14")
assert the radio's absence without ever having selected it, so `values.rule` is
still `fixed` and the branch never runs. The line is untested.

**The house pattern.** `components/entity-form-dialog.tsx:122-129` does the same
kind of derived-during-render correction but guards it with a `useState`
"previous" tracker so it fires once on a transition rather than on every render
that satisfies the condition. Applying that shape here bounds the write to the
moment the offer actually changes. I will not pretend it makes React happy in
every case — it still writes during render — so the honest ranking is: **the
missing test is the thing worth adding first**, and the guard second.

**What I am not proposing.** Clearing the rule inside the category
`onValueChange` (beside the existing `clearTheAnswer()`) would move the write
into an event handler where React has no objection, but it would reset the rule
on *every* category change, not only when the chosen one is withdrawn. That is a
behaviour change and out of scope here.

**Blast radius.** One component, plus one test.

## 9 — `would_ask === 0` stands in for "the category has nothing registered", and is not exact

## What I looked at and decided was NOT worth the churn

- **`_can_be_spread`'s second recurrence walk.** Real and measured: 3 walks per
  obligation-pass where there used to be 1, and 57 walks for a 13-month fold of
  one fund. But none of it is a query — the read path's budget under ADR-0028 is
  statements, and those are flat at 14 whether one fund is read or five. The
  extra walk buys correctness for cadences that do not divide into months
  ("every 45 days", "every 6 weeks"), which the docstring states as the reason.
  Leave it.
- **`has_repeating_charges=bool(agg.obligations_in(fund.category_id))`.** Asked
  once per fund in `fold`, and `obligations_in` is an in-memory list
  comprehension over `active_recurring` — no query, confirmed by reading
  `month_aggregate.py`. Building the list only to test it for emptiness costs
  one allocation; `any(...)` would save it and nothing else.
- **`_crowded` and `_warning` taking a whole `Fund` to read `start_month`.**
  A decoupling nobody is being hurt by.
- **The generator emitting `('Given', …), ('And', …), ('Given', …)`** — the
  background's keywords followed by the scenario's own `Given`. It reads oddly
  and matches nothing, because keywords are not used for lookup. The background
  placement itself is right: I checked the emitted module and the two background
  steps lead every plain scenario *and* every one of the four expanded rows of
  the AC-13 outline, before the substituted steps. Not substituting placeholders
  into background steps is also correct — standard Gherkin substitutes only
  inside a Scenario Outline's own steps.
- **`_lines`' `getattr(status, "charges", None)`** — red-phase scaffolding now
  that the field exists and defaults to `[]`. One line.
- **`then_records_hold_no_breakdown` banning every table whose name contains
  "fund"** — broader than the criterion, but it errs safe, which is the right
  direction for that guard.
- **The Spanish announcement in `create-form.tsx` duplicating the English one in
  `_warning`.** Deliberate, blessed by ADR-0054 and stated in both docstrings:
  the service hands over the charge and each surface words it. The figure is the
  same on both sides — checked.
