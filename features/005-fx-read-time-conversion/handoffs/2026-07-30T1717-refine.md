## What I deliberately left alone, and why

- **`/planned/to-pay` converts each row twice** (from_tx lists + 
  `total_cop_cents`): fixing it would orphan `OutstandingQueue.
  total_cop_cents` (a unit-tested domain API) for a marginal gain on
  dozens of rows. Rejected as churn > value.
- **Memoizing `MonthAggregate.to_cop_cents`**: within the <100 ms budget
  (informative, not a gate); adds hidden state. Rejected.
- **`transfer(date=None)` footgun** (Quality F4): cannot be restored to
  required without breaking positional callers — Python forbids a
  non-default param after the defaulted `currency`, and reordering or
  keyword-only breaks the REST/MCP/acceptance call sites. Left as-is;
  CP7/feature-edit may weigh an early `ValidationError` (that would be a
  behavior change, hence out of refine's scope).
- **`acceptance/handlers/fx_read_time.py` stale tense** ("the feature is
  not implemented yet…", "intended API"): my constraints allow touching
  handlers only if a rename forces it; none did. Recommend CP7/CP8 clean
  this docstring drift under their mandate.
- **`TransferReceivedField` double gate** (`!active ||` same-currency):
  the same-currency null render is a test-locked component contract;
  removing the internal check would mean deleting a test case. Kept.
- **Settings singleton id spelled in several places** (fx.py `_SETTINGS_ID`,
  literal `1` in settings/goals/planned): unifying properly touches
  unchanged files; partial adoption would add a 4th spelling. Follow-up.
- **`fxRate` zod primitive name** (lib/schemas/primitives.ts): unchanged
  file, out of the feature's blast radius. Follow-up rename to `trm`.
- **MCP `# ----- section -----` markers** in tools/core.py/temporal.py and
  the `_resolve_account` legacy aliases: pre-existing repo style; the
  alias cleanup would touch 3 unchanged tool modules. Repo-wide follow-up.
- **Private-helper docstrings** kept: the charter prohibits comments;
  docstrings are the sanctioned documentation form and deleting them
  loses information.
