# refine (round 2) — handoff summary

## Why this ran at all

The Principle 7 gate had been red since 2026-08-09 because `main-session`
implemented CP5 and then also applied CP6's findings. The owner chose to repair
that rather than document it, on the argument that the code had been patched
about thirty times in one day, each patch looking at one symptom, and nobody had
read the result whole.

That turned out to be the right call by a wide margin.

## What was done

Three fresh review agents over the merged diff, one per lens. Their 39 findings
were consolidated and charter-filtered, and every one that claimed a defect was
reproduced by hand — over HTTP, against today's production data restored into a
scratch database, with the API on a spare port and the scheduler off. Three of
the reported defects did not survive that step and were triaged out. Six did.

Four fresh agents then applied the fixes and the refactor. `main-session` wrote
no code; it wrote the briefs, and re-ran its own reproductions after every stage.

## What the numbers do not prove

`services/metas.py` sits at 99% line coverage and CRAP finds no backend function
above threshold. Six user-facing defects were found in exactly that code. Line
coverage says the lines ran; it does not say the amounts they produced were right
for a month no test names.
