# refine — handoff summary

## What I did

Dispatched three fresh review subagents over the branch's changed code — reuse,

twelve surviving findings. Two of them were real bugs in code that a full green

## Artifacts produced

## Findings

### The two bugs the reviewers found

**Closing a running meta evaporated its money.** `close_meta` archived any meta,
bought or not, and releases nothing by design (AC-39). Archiving a *running*
meta set `archived=True` with `cancelled_month=None`, and `month_aggregate`
keeps a row only when `archived is False OR cancelled_month >= year_month` —
`NULL >= '2026-12'` is `NULL`. The meta disappeared from every later month
holding nothing and handing nothing back, and the month's identity silently
stopped balancing. For a *bought* meta that behaviour is exactly right, which is
why the missing guard was invisible. `restore_meta` had the mirror hole.

Both rules did exist — in `meta-actions.tsx` and `archived-list.tsx`. The
service, where a refusal belongs and where `cancel_meta` already keeps its own,
had neither. The asymmetry was the tell.

Falsified before fixing: removing the close guard fails
`test_closing_a_meta_still_running_is_refused_so_its_money_cannot_evaporate`
with 200 instead of 422.

### The regression I introduced

`61fa014` moved the month's read path out of `services/funds.py` into
`services/month.py`. `acceptance/handlers/` is **committed source**, not
generated, and it binds to service names: `funds._month_view`, `funds.split`,
`funds.available`, `funds.month_available`. All of them stopped resolving.
121 of 472 acceptance tests went red and stayed red across two commits.

I reported green twice on two signals that could not see it:

- `spec_coverage.py` reporting `unbound 0` — which checks that a test *carrying
  each scenario's name exists*, and nothing about whether it passes.
- the backend unit suite at 1012 — which never imports the handlers.

The handlers are now bound to the module that owns the behaviour, and
`sinking_funds.py`'s dynamic `_call` resolves a capability across both the fund
and month services rather than in whichever module held it first — a scenario
pins behaviour and has no business knowing which module implements it.

### What was declined, and why

Two on the charter (a code comment, prohibited project-wide; renaming three
shipped URLs) and two on measurement — `_walk` memoisation saves 0.22 ms of an
8.3 ms request, and the fund fold is 63 ms of a 67 ms read path at 30 funds over
120 months, so walking it twice to tidy the cheap half was the wrong trade. The
seam is `funds.fold(agg) -> FundFold` off one walk instead.

### The query bound

`BOUNDED_LOADS` went 14 → 13: the month's expense window and its income window
differed only by `type`, and one statement partitioned in memory returns the
same rows. ADR-0046 and `plan.md` both still claimed 14, and ADR-0046's
Consequences bullet had claimed "rises by two" since its first draft, in
contradiction with its own read-path section. All three now carry a dated
correction rather than a rewrite.
